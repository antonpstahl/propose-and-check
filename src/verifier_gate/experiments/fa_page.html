E2 False-Alarm Labels

Sentences from texts the gate released (E3) that a fresh reading by the extractor would block. For each claim the reading found: does the sentence really say it?

How to label

The highlighted sentence was read by the extractor, which found the claims listed under it. The verifier says each claim is not supported by the facts, and the reason is shown. Your job is to judge the reading: does the sentence say this?

Real error
The sentence says this, or something that means the same, and the facts do not support it. The block is right.
Misreading
The sentence does not say this. What it does say is fine, so blocking it would be a false alarm.
Unsure
You cannot tell. Add a note if you can say why.

If you think the sentence says this but the facts do support it, choose Unsure and write why: that would be a question for the lexicon, not the reader. "Not covered" items ask whether the text says something supported about that feature anywhere.

Keys: E real error, M misreading, U unsure (each labels the highlighted claim and moves on), ← → previous and next sentence. Labels save as you go.
