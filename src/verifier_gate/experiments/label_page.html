E2 Mutant Labels

100 sentences that Claude Haiku 5.5 rewrote in explanations the gate had released (E3, extractor x3). Label each one against the facts alone.

How to label

Judge the new sentence as a whole, against the facts on the right and the word meanings in the key. The facts are the ones the mutator saw (handover H1). The gate's verdict and the mutator's own label are not on this page.

Error
It says something false about the model, or more than the facts support: a class word the share band does not fit, a rank or a location the facts do not give, a hedge on something the bands settle.
No error
Everything it says is supported by the facts, even if it is vaguer than before or only reworded.
Unsure
You cannot tell from these facts. Add a note if you can say why.

Keys: E error, N no error, U unsure (each moves to the next item), ← → previous and next. Labels save as you go.
