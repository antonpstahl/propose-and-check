Critic Flag Labels

Sentences from texts the gate released (E3) that the LLM critic would block. For each problem the critic names: is the sentence really unsupported by the facts?

How to label

The LLM critic says the highlighted sentence states something the facts do not support, and gives its reason. Your job is to judge the critic: read the sentence against the facts on the right.

Real error
The sentence states something the facts do not support: false, or more than the bands allow. The block is right.
False alarm
What the sentence says is supported by the facts. Blocking it would be a false alarm, whatever the critic's reason.
Unsure
You cannot tell. Add a note if you can say why.

Judge by the meanings under What the words mean (the lexicon), as for the earlier labels. If the critic is right by a reasonable reading of the words but the lexicon accepts the sentence, choose Unsure and start the note with lexicon: that is where readers and the lexicon differ.

Judge the sentence, not the critic's wording: if the sentence is unsupported for another reason than the one given, it is still a real error; add a note. Items for the whole text (no sentence highlighted) say the text fails to mention a feature or pair: is that feature among the three most important, or a pair with a share of at least 10%, and missing from the text?

Keys: E real error, F false alarm, U unsure (each labels the highlighted problem and moves on), ← → previous and next sentence. Labels save as you go.
